Piczy – Privacy Policy

Developer: Osli software · Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains what information the photo puzzle Piczy ("the game") uses when you play it on a web game site, on YouTube Playables, or in the Piczy app.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your progress (solved photos, stars, daily streak, settings and the puzzle you are playing) is saved only on your own device or, on sites that offer it, in that site's own save feature. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

2. Your own photos

If you choose "My photo", the picture is cut into puzzle pieces on your device. It is never uploaded, stored on a server or shared with anyone.

3. Web game sites and YouTube

When you play on a game website (for example CrazyGames, Poki, Playgama, GameDistribution or itch.io) or on YouTube Playables, that site may show its own ads (for example a short ad after you finish a puzzle) and collect information under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing to us.

4. The Piczy Android app

The Android app may show ads through Google AdMob and offers an optional one-time purchase through Google Play. Google may collect information such as your advertising ID and IP address for ads, under Google's privacy policy. You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads in your Android settings.

5. Children

The game is a quiet puzzle with no chat, no accounts and no personal data collection. Ads shown by a web site or app store are controlled by that platform.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
