Grid Copy: Draw by Squares – Privacy Policy

Developer: Osli software · Last updated: 16 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Grid Copy: Draw by Squares ("the game") uses, on Android and on web game sites.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your progress (stars, unlocked levels, chosen pen, sound setting) and your best drawings for the sketchbook are saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes them. Your drawings are never sent anywhere.

2. Advertising

The current Android version shows no ads and contains no advertising code. If a later version adds ads (for example Google AdMob), this policy will be updated before that version is released, to explain what information the ad provider uses and how you can control it.

3. Web game sites and YouTube

When you play on a game website (for example itch.io or Playgama), that site may show its own ads and collect information under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing to us.

4. Crash reports

If the Android app crashes, Google Play may send an anonymous crash report to the developer through the Google Play Console. It does not identify you.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell or share personal information.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
