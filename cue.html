Cue – Privacy Policy

Developer: Osli software · Last updated: 6 November 2026

This policy explains what information the Android app Cue ("the app"), an offline player for your own music, audiobooks and lectures, uses.

1. Information the app collects

None. Cue has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything anywhere. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics and no servers.

2. What stays on your phone

To work, Cue reads the audio files on your phone (and any folder you choose to add) and keeps its own notes about them on your device only: your playlists, books and courses, the second you stopped at in each file, playback speed, bookmarks, favourites and settings. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes all of this.

Cue asks only for access to audio. It cannot see your photos, contacts or other files. Deleting, renaming or moving a file always goes through Android's own confirmation.

3. Backups

If you use Back up, Cue writes a small file to a folder you pick. If that folder is synced to a cloud service, that service handles the file under its own privacy policy. Cue itself never uploads anything.

4. Purchases

The one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under its own privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy). We receive no payment details.

5. Crash reports

If the app crashes, Google Play may send an anonymous crash report to the developer through the Google Play Console. It does not identify you.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It collects no information from anyone.

7. Sharing and selling

We do not collect, sell or share personal information.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
