Ironhound: Robot Dog Shooter – Privacy Policy

Developer: Osli software · Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy explains what information the game Ironhound: Robot Dog Shooter ("the game") uses, on Android and on web game sites.

1. Information the game itself collects

None. The game has no accounts, no sign-in and no servers of its own. Your progress (missions, stars, upgrades, settings) is saved only on your own device. Uninstalling the game or clearing its data deletes it.

2. Advertising (Android)

The free Android version shows ads through Google AdMob: a full-screen ad between missions (at most once every few minutes, never during play) and an optional "watch an ad to double your scrap" button. Google may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, device type and how you interact with ads, to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud.

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland the game asks for your consent with Google's consent form before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time with Privacy options on the game's title screen.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • How Google uses this information: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Web game sites and YouTube

When you play on a game website (for example CrazyGames, Poki or itch.io) or on YouTube Playables, that site may show its own ads and collect information under its own privacy policy. The game itself sends nothing to us.

4. Crash reports

If the Android app crashes, Google Play may send an anonymous crash report to the developer through the Google Play Console. It does not identify you.

5. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for them. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

6. Sharing and selling

We do not sell personal information and we do not share it with anyone, apart from the advertising described above.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
